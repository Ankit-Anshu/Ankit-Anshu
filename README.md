## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am **Ankit Anshu**, a **Data Analyst** with a background in Computer Science Engineering.

Over the past few years, my interest in data has grown through projects that turn raw, unstructured information into something more meaningful. From exploring a dataset and finding patterns to understanding what those patterns actually mean, I enjoy the process of making sense of data and communicating it clearly.

My work and projects have given me experience with SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, along with data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and reporting. I am particularly interested in business problems where thoughtful analysis can bring clarity to a decision or reveal something that might otherwise be missed.

Alongside my analytics work, I contribute to the **Codebasics community** by helping learners resolve doubts, troubleshoot technical issues, and understand concepts related to data analytics and data engineering.

I am also independently building **Meisai**, a browser workspace for focused work and learning. What started as a personal idea has grown into a project where I'm exploring product design, understanding user needs, and turning an idea into something practical and usable.
